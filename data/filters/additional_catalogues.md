APASS DR9: II/336/apass9
https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/II/336/ReadMe
B and V use Johnson Vega magnitudes; gri use AB magnitudes.
The filter wavelengths follow Cifuentes et al. (2020), Table 1 (APASS).

SkyMapper DR4: II/379/smssdr4
https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/II/379/ReadMe
Weighted mean PSF magnitudes, in the AB system.
Central wavelengths in catalogue_bands.csv are from Onken et al. (2024),
Table 1: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02015
These are central wavelengths, not SVO effective wavelengths.
No SDSS transmission curve is substituted for a SkyMapper filter.
SkyMapper is not yet included in VOSA export: its VOSA filter mapping
must be verified when the SVO service is accessible.
